**Sample post (Week 1, Monday — launch):**
> For 12 years, my job was to keep everything running.
>
> Calendars. Deadlines. Crises that never made it into anyone's job description.
>
> I was good at it. But I watched something over those years that I can't unsee:
> workplaces rarely break people loudly.
> They break people quietly — one skipped lunch, one 7 pm "quick favor," one blurred boundary at a time.
>
> The World Health Organization estimates 12 billion working days are lost every year to depression and anxiety. That number isn't made in hospitals. It's made in offices like the ones I've spent my career in.
>
> So I'm starting something new — here, publicly.
> I'll be sharing practical, evidence-informed ways to manage stress, prevent burnout, and build work routines that protect people. Systems, not slogans.
>
> One thing I'll always be clear about: I'm not a therapist, and this isn't therapy. It's education — the part that comes *before* someone needs professional help, and the honesty to point to real help when it's needed.
>
> If you work in Lebanon and any of this sounds like your office — stay close.
>
> #WorkplaceWellbeing #MentalHealthLiteracy #BurnoutPrevention #Lebanon

## Operating notes

1. **Batching:** each month's posts are drafted in the last week of the prior month via `PREMIUM_PROMPT_LIBRARY.md` §B, then pass the Friday approval gates week by week.
2. **Flexibility:** national events or crises in Lebanon override the calendar — pause scheduled posts and let Dorina decide tone (sensitivity protocol in `CONTENT_APPROVAL_AND_AUTOMATION_WORKFLOW.md` §6).
3. **Recycling:** from month 7, top posts are refreshed and repurposed to Instagram (`INSTAGRAM_EXTENSION_ENGINE.md`).
4. **Measurement:** first Friday of each month = analytics review against the KPI table in `LINKEDIN_AUTHORITY_ENGINE.md` §5.
5. **Composite stories:** any "colleague" story is anonymized or composite and labeled as such — never a real identifiable person without written consent.
