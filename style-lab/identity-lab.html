Directions — the mark

A · recommended

The Open Circle

The unclosed ring — every circle has a door. The gold signal sits in the opening: noticing it is what completes the circle. One gesture that holds CIRCLE, DESK, and the choice-based safety promise.
A2

Open Circle · monogram

Same ring carrying the serif D — for avatars and seals where the name isn't beside it. Reversed on forest.
A3 · bilingual

Open Circle · دال

The Arabic dāl inside the ring — an identity that leads Arabic-first for the Lebanon launch. Pairs 1:1 with A2 (D) so both scripts share one system.
D

The Seven Days

Seven points forming the circle; the seventh — gold — is the practice that continues after the room. Quieter, more abstract; strong as a pattern/motif even if not the master mark.
B

The Seal

Engraved double-ring monogram — classic printed authority. Best on certificates and workbook covers; least distinctive as an avatar.
C

The Signal in the Name

Wordmark ending in the gold point. Elegant and minimal; weak at small sizes, gives programs nothing to inherit.

How to choose: A is the working recommendation because it scales from favicon to flyer and both programs inherit it. A2/A3 are its avatar forms, not competitors. D can live alongside A as a pattern. B and C are true alternatives.
