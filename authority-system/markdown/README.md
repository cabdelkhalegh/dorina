# Dorina Salman — Mental Health Management Authority Package

**Prepared for:** Dorina Salman (Lebanon)
**Package purpose:** A complete, ready-to-execute strategy to build Dorina's public authority in **mental health management** — meaning practical, non-clinical workplace wellbeing education: stress literacy, burnout prevention, boundaries, routines, emotional organization, and help-seeking.
**Date:** July 2026

---

## What this package is (and is not)

This package positions Dorina as a **workplace wellbeing educator and mental health literacy advocate** — a bridge between administrative systems expertise and workplace wellbeing.

It deliberately does **not** position Dorina as a therapist, psychologist, counselor, clinician, or treatment provider, because no clinical credential is on file. Every document in this package is built on that boundary. Educational content only; not therapy, diagnosis, crisis support, medical, legal, or HR/legal advice. Crisis situations are always referred to emergency services or qualified professionals.

This boundary is not a limitation — it is the strategy. The world's most trusted workplace wellbeing voices are educators and advocates, and the WHO explicitly recommends worker mental health literacy, awareness-building, and stress-management skills training as core workplace actions. That is exactly the lane this package builds.

## The strategy in one paragraph

Dorina rebuilds her LinkedIn presence around one clear promise — *practical mental health management for working professionals in Lebanon and the region* — using her 12+ years of administrative and executive-support experience as her unique lens: **she knows how workplaces actually run**, and she translates wellbeing science communicated by authorities like the WHO into systems, routines, and habits people can actually use at a desk. She earns authority through consistent educational content (3 posts/week), then converts attention into non-clinical workshops for companies and teams, supported by partnerships with HR communities and organizations aligned with Lebanon's National Mental Health Programme ecosystem. Every piece of content and every workshop carries clear educational boundaries and referral pathways.
