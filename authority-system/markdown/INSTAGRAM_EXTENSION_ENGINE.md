# Instagram Extension Engine — Dorina Salman

**Purpose:** The phase-2 channel. Instagram launches **only after LinkedIn is stable** (target: Month 7, ~February–March 2027, per the roadmap) and runs primarily as a **repurposing engine** — LinkedIn authority content, reshaped for a younger, more visual, more Arabic-first audience. It must never double Dorina's workload: the rule is *create once on LinkedIn, adapt twice for Instagram*.

Everything here inherits the same boundaries: educational content only — never therapy, diagnosis, or crisis support; referral signposting always present; **no post publishes without Dorina's approval of copy and visual.**

---

## 1. Why Instagram second (not first, not never)

- **LinkedIn first** because the buyers (HR, managers, SME owners) and the professional-authority context live there.
- **Instagram second** because: (1) stigma-reduction and literacy content reaches young Lebanese professionals and the diaspora far better there; (2) workshop moments (photos, reels) are inherently visual; (3) WHO framing — mental health as a basic human right, literacy and awareness as recommended actions — deserves the wider, more personal channel; (4) future products (toolkits, open workshops) will sell to individuals, and individuals live on Instagram.
- **Never before it's sustainable:** launch criteria below are hard gates.

**Launch gates (all must be true):**
1. LinkedIn cadence ≥90% consistency for 3 consecutive months.
2. At least 4 workshops delivered (so there is real visual material).
3. Visual identity finalized (palette, templates, Arabic typography).
4. Dorina confirms the extra ~30 min/week of approval time.

## 3. Content formats & mix

Cadence: **2 feed posts + 2–3 stories per week** (deliberately modest), 4 reels/month maximum.

| Format | Share | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Carousels** | 40% | Direct adaptation of LinkedIn carousels (DESK how-tos, WHO risk lists, boundary scripts) | Redesign to IG ratio (4:5), tighter text, Arabic-first versions prioritized |
| **Reels (30–60s)** | 30% | Talking-head micro-lessons; workshop clips (with participant consent); text-on-screen tips | Hook in first 2 seconds; captions always (silent viewing); bilingual subtitles |
| **Quote/stat cards** | 15% | WHO-attributed stats and Dorina's own aphorisms | Attribution on the image itself, not just the caption |
| **Stories** | (weekly) | Behind-the-scenes, polls, question stickers, workshop days | Polls/questions double as content research for LinkedIn |
| **Personal/advocacy posts** | 15% | Story pillar; stigma-reduction; Arabic-first | Highest resonance format for the Lebanese/diaspora audience |

**Arabic strategy:** Instagram is the Arabic-first channel. Target ≥50% Arabic or bilingual content (vs. LinkedIn's English-lean). Advocacy and stigma content leads in Arabic; systems content can stay bilingual.

## 4. The repurposing pipeline (create once, adapt twice)

Weekly flow, attached to the existing Thursday–Friday production rhythm:

1. **Select:** from this week's LinkedIn posts (and the archive), pick the 2 best candidates for IG (criteria: visual potential, emotional resonance, evergreen value).
2. **Adapt copy:** shorten to IG caption norms — hook line, 3–6 short lines, question or soft CTA, hashtags (8–12 mixed AR/EN, e.g., #الصحة_النفسية #WorkplaceWellbeing #Lebanon #BurnoutPrevention).
3. **Adapt visual:** re-layout to 4:5 or 9:16 using the IG template set (`PREMIUM_PROMPT_LIBRARY.md` §G4–G5).
4. **Approval gate:** copy + visual reviewed and approved by Dorina alongside the LinkedIn batch (same Friday session, ~30 extra minutes).
5. **Schedule:** approved posts only; posting days Tuesday + Saturday (offset from LinkedIn days to spread presence).
6. **Engage:** 10 min/day, 3 days/week — replies, story interactions. DMs disclosing personal distress get the referral response from `RISK_ETHICS_AND_REFERRAL_PROTOCOL.md`, verbatim, always.

Reels production is batched monthly: one recording session (4 reels), scripts pre-approved, edited clips re-approved before posting.

## 6. Channel-specific risk notes

- **Younger, more vulnerable audience:** IG comments/DMs are likelier to include personal disclosures. The referral response script is mandatory, unmodified; no advice-giving in DMs, ever. Comment moderation: hide abusive content, never argue publicly.
- **Aesthetic drift:** IG rewards "wellness aesthetics" — resist lotus-and-beach clichés; keep the calm-authority identity (`POSITIONING_AND_BRAND_ARCHITECTURE.md` §10) so the brand stays educational, not lifestyle.
- **Workshop footage:** only with written participant consent (consent line built into workshop registration — see `WORKSHOP_SYSTEM_BLUEPRINT.md` §7); default to audience-free angles.
- **Every advocacy post carries the short disclaimer** in caption or image footer: educational content — not therapy or crisis support; seek qualified help / emergency services if struggling.
