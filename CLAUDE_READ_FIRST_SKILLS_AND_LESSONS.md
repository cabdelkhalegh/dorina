## 3. Self-improvement lessons learned in this Dorina work

### Lesson 1 — Do not change production design before mockup approval

AK explicitly objected when production style changed without approval. For Dorina:

- Experiment in `style-lab/` first.
- Keep `/`, `/women-circle/`, and `/authority-system/` unchanged unless AK explicitly approves.
- Verify public pages after each mockup commit.

### Lesson 2 — Old approved visual language matters more than new taste

AK attached the old design and said that was the style. Preserve:

- forest-green sidebar;
- cream/off-white canvas;
- large editorial serif hierarchy;
- calm serious authority;
- restrained UI, not shiny SaaS;
- Dorina Salman authority identity.

Current old-design mockup candidate:

`style-lab/authority-old-design-workflow.html`

### Lesson 3 — Motion must be semantic, not decorative

Earlier motion was still too flat because it was mostly ambience. Better motion:

- shows selection changing;
- shows approval/blocker/progress state;
- supports workflow continuity;
- does not distract or make the product feel fake.

### Lesson 4 — Product realism beats concept-board beauty

The big platform must feel like a real operating platform:

- document library;
- selected object state;
- evidence/source review;
- named approvers/owners;
- blocker reasons;
- audit trail;
- export readiness;
- mobile navigation that is designed for phone, not squeezed desktop.

Current workflow candidate:

`style-lab/authority-old-design-workflow.html`

Previous Mobbin-style workflow candidate:

`style-lab/authority-workflow-loop.html`

### Lesson 5 — Framework/IP claims must be grounded

Use the handoff:

`CLAUDE_APP_DORINA_FRAMEWORKS_HANDOFF.md`

Do not claim:

- legal trademark registration;
- clinical evidence;
- treatment/therapy outcomes;

Use:

### Lesson 6 — Keep CIRCLE and DESK separate

- **CIRCLE** = women/private/group wellbeing journey.
- **DESK** = workplace/corporate/team journey.
- **CLEAR** = safety container usable in both.
- **Bad-Day Card / Trigger Map / Support Compass** = mainly CIRCLE tools.
