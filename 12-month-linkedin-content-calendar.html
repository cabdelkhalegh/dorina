Sample post (Week 1, Monday — launch):

For 12 years, my job was to keep everything running.

Calendars. Deadlines. Crises that never made it into anyone's job description.

I was good at it. But I watched something over those years that I can't unsee: workplaces rarely break people loudly. They break people quietly — one skipped lunch, one 7 pm "quick favor," one blurred boundary at a time.

The World Health Organization estimates 12 billion working days are lost every year to depression and anxiety. That number isn't made in hospitals. It's made in offices like the ones I've spent my career in.

So I'm starting something new — here, publicly. I'll be sharing practical, evidence-informed ways to manage stress, prevent burnout, and build work routines that protect people. Systems, not slogans.

One thing I'll always be clear about: I'm not a therapist, and this isn't therapy. It's education — the part that comes before someone needs professional help, and the honesty to point to real help when it's needed.

If you work in Lebanon and any of this sounds like your office — stay close.

اليوم هو اليوم العالمي للصحة النفسية. — Today is World Mental Health Day.

The WHO tells us that 15% of working-age adults were living with a mental disorder in 2019 — and that effective workplace actions exist: awareness, literacy, manager training, stress-management skills.

Lebanon has been here before, officially: our Ministry of Public Health ran a national workplace campaign with one unforgettable slogan — ما تضيعوا الفرصة. Don't miss the opportunity.
