Profile

I help women and working teams notice pressure early, name what they feel, and choose one useful next step.

I spent eight years in executive administration, in a law firm and a group CEO’s office, so I know how pressure builds at a busy desk. My sessions turn that into short, practical tools people can use the same week.

My work is educational and non-clinical. I do not diagnose or treat any condition. When someone needs qualified professional care, I say so and help them find it.

Ways to work together

Women in Power Circle

A three-hour women’s wellbeing circle for up to 15 women, built on the CIRCLE Method. Each woman leaves with a printed Arabic and English kit and gets a seven-day WhatsApp follow-up.

Hosted circle: the same three hours for your studio, association, NGO staff group or women’s network. You bring the room and the women; I bring the method, the run of the session and a kit for every woman.

Educational sessions for teams, by enquiry, in Arabic or English:

  • DESK Online team session: 90 minutes online, up to 25 people
  • The Supportive Manager: 2 hours, 6 to 15 managers
  • The DESK half day: 3.5 to 4 hours on site, 10 to 20 people

Teams: start with a free 45-minute taster, one per company, on request.

Methods

The CIRCLE Method

six moves from the first body signal to one chosen next step.

  1. Come back to the body
  2. Identify the emotion
  3. Read the trigger
  4. Choose the response
  5. Link to support
  6. Embed the practice

The take-home kit: Trigger Map Support Map Bad-Day Card Seven-day Integration

The DESK Method

  1. Detect
  2. Establish
  3. Support
  4. Keep

Early signals, boundaries, visible workload, and help-seeking as a professional skill.
