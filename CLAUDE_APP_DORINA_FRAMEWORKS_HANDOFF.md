# 3. New framework candidates to create without hallucination

These are **new proposed brand assets**, not existing facts and not registered marks. They are derived from the current package language, tools, and safety boundaries.

## 1. The CLEAR Container™ — safety and facilitation framework

**Why it exists:** The package already has a “container” concept: predictable agenda, opt-out rights, no forced sharing, confidentiality norm, and exit ramp. This framework gives it a memorable name.

| Letter | Meaning | Operational rule |
|---|---|---|
| C | Choice | Participation is invited, never forced |
| L | Limits | The room is education, not therapy or diagnosis |
| E | Exit | People can pause, pass, or step out without explanation |
| A | Agreements | Confidentiality, respectful listening, no advice-giving |
| R | Referral | Needs outside scope move to qualified support pathways |

**Best use:** Opening slide, facilitator guide, client proposal, participant workbook.  
**Risk:** Must not sound like trauma/therapy containment. Keep it “facilitation container” and educational.

---

## 2. The Friday 15™ — sustaining-habit framework

**Why it exists:** The Friday 15 Weekly Review already exists in the workshop blueprint. Turn it into a repeatable Dorina-owned ritual.

| Minute | Action | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | Detect | What signal showed up this week? |
| 3–7 | Establish | What boundary/workload adjustment is needed? |
| 7–11 | Support | Who or what support is needed next week? |
| 11–15 | Keep | What one habit gets protected? |

**Best use:** LinkedIn carousel series, corporate handout, follow-up email, recurring client retainer.  
**Risk:** Very practical and likely ownable through consistent Dorina usage, but legal search still required.

## 3. The Workload X-Ray™ — workplace visibility tool

**Why it exists:** Workload X-Ray already exists as a DESK exercise. It should become a named diagnostic worksheet without becoming a psychological assessment.

| Layer | Question | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Visible load | What is already on the calendar/task list? | Visible task list |
| Invisible load | What work is being carried but not seen? | Hidden-load notes |
| Interruption load | What keeps breaking focus? | Interruption pattern |
| Control level | What can/cannot be changed? | Control map |
| Next adjustment | What is the smallest structural change? | One action |

**Best use:** Corporate teams, admin professionals, managers.  
**Risk:** Call it a visibility tool, not an assessment.

## 4. The Bad-Day Card™ — personal carry-home tool

**Why it exists:** Bad-Day Card already exists in CIRCLE. Make it Dorina’s most memorable take-home artifact.

| Part | Content |

**Risk:** Avoid “panic card”, “crisis card”, or treatment framing.

## 6. The Quiet Breakpoint™ — signature content concept

**Why it exists:** Dorina’s story says workplaces often break people quietly: skipped lunch, blurred boundaries, invisible load. This can become a branded teaching concept.

**Definition:** The moment when repeated small pressures become an unsustainable pattern — before crisis, before diagnosis, before collapse.

| Signal area | Examples |
|---|---|
| Body | jaw, shoulders, breath, stomach, fatigue |
| Time | no white space, always late, no recovery |
| Workload | invisible work, interruption load, unclear priorities |
| Emotion | irritability, dread, numbness, overwhelm |
| Support | no ask made, wrong person asked, silence |

**Best use:** LinkedIn flagship essays, keynote opening, manager training.  
**Risk:** Do not define it as a clinical threshold. It is an educational observation concept.
