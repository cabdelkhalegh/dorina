## The system
- **Primary lockup:** `dorina-lockup.svg` — Open Circle symbol + script signature. Use whenever space allows.
- **Signature alone:** `dorina-signature.svg` — editorial contexts where the symbol appears elsewhere.
- **Symbol alone:** `open-circle.svg` / `open-circle-reversed.svg` — favicon, avatars, stamps, small spaces.
- **Favicon:** `favicon.svg` (heavier stroke for 16–32 px).
- **LinkedIn banner:** `banner-linkedin.png` (1584×396, generated from `banner-linkedin.html`).

## Meaning
The ring is deliberately unclosed — every circle has a door (choice, pass, exit).
The gold point sits in the opening: noticing the signal is what completes the circle.

## Type
- Script (the signature): **Monsieur La Doulaise** — name only, never sentences.
- Display serif: **Cormorant Garamond** — headlines.
- UI/body: **Inter** · Arabic: **IBM Plex Sans Arabic**.

## Rules
1. The signature is always forest or ink — never gold, never outlined.
2. The gold dot is the only gold shape; gold otherwise appears as hairlines.
3. The mark never sits beside clinical language (therapy, treatment, diagnosis).
4. Clear space around the lockup = height of the symbol's gold dot ×4.
5. Script SVGs use the webfont; for print, convert text to outlines — or replace with Dorina's traced real signature (planned upgrade; symbol and rules unchanged).
6. Women in Power Circle may warm the canvas with sage `#AFBBA1` / rose `#E7C6CD` as lane accents; the master lockup itself does not change.
