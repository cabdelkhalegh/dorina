## The redirect rule

Every LinkedIn post should do three things:

1. name one recognisable everyday moment;
2. teach one small CIRCLE move without trying to teach the entire program;
3. use one CTA that sends the reader to the matching landing-page section.

Do not stack several asks in one post. A post either invites reflection, sends to the method, or opens an inquiry.

## Six content pillars tied to CIRCLE

| CIRCLE move | LinkedIn teaching angle | Example hook | Landing anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Come back to the body | body signals and breath-led pauses | “The first sign of pressure may be in your jaw, pace, or shoulders—not in your words.” | `#method` or `#tools` |
| Identify the emotion | precise emotion language | “A clearer emotion word can make the next request easier to name.” | `#method` |
| Read the trigger | five-box Trigger Map | “A repeated reaction becomes easier to read when we separate the situation, body signal, emotion, need, and response.” | `#tools` |
| Choose the response | boundary phrases and response options | “A useful boundary can be one true sentence, not a long defence.” | `#tools` |
| Link to support | exact support asks | “A list of names becomes a Support Map only when each name has a clear ask.” | `#tools` |
| Embed the practice | Bad-Day Card and seven days | “A tool becomes more useful when it has a place in an ordinary week.” | `#integration` |

For the live URL, append the anchor after the page URL, for example:

`https://cabdelkhalegh.github.io/dorina/women-circle/landing.html#tools`

## Inquiry handoff

The current landing page prepares an inquiry message locally in the visitor’s browser. It does not upload entries. Until Dorina approves a public WhatsApp, email, or booking endpoint, the visitor copies or shares the prepared message through Dorina’s LinkedIn link or the confirmed contact channel.

When a real endpoint is approved, keep the same fields and replace only the final handoff:

- name;
- contact identity;
- preferred path;
- Arabic/English preference;
- one short context sentence.

## Seven-day and feedback loop back to LinkedIn

1. Dorina delivers the agreed format using the six CIRCLE moves.
2. Participants receive one short integration prompt per day.
3. Dorina asks which tool was used, what was clear, and what needs adjustment.
4. Any public quote requires separate approval of the exact wording.
5. Dorina turns recurring anonymised questions—not private stories—into the next educational posts.
6. Those posts link back to the matching CIRCLE section and the next inquiry opportunity.

This is how Dorina’s authority grows: repeated useful teaching, consistent delivery, approved experience feedback, and visible continuity—not inflated claims.

## Weekly operating review

Track only signals that improve the journey:

- which posts create relevant landing-page visits;
- which CIRCLE section people reference in inquiries;
- preferred path and language;
- response time;
- inquiry-to-conversation and conversation-to-booking rates;
- which take-home tools participants report as clear or usable;
- repeat interest, referrals, and hosted-circle inquiries;
- the next content question and the next delivery improvement.

## Four-week decision rule

If four weeks pass with no qualified inquiries after the LinkedIn link is published, do not keep posting the same CTA. Review:
1. Is the headline clear enough?
2. Is the first screen of the landing page emotionally specific enough?
3. Is the contact method easy enough?
4. Are posts speaking to real Lebanon-audience situations, or generic wellbeing language?
5. Should the first offer be a smaller paid/private consultation before a public circle?
