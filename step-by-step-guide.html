Step-by-Step Guide: How to Use Dorina’s Authority Package

Recommended rule: do not upload, publish, send, or automate anything before Dorina approves the positioning and safety language.

Start with the Executive Summary

Open Executive Summary for Dorina. Dorina should understand the big idea: she is positioned as a non-clinical workplace wellbeing educator / mental health literacy advocate, not as a therapist or treatment provider.

Approve the safety frame first

Open Risk, Ethics & Referral Protocol. Confirm the boundaries, disclaimers, referral language, and crisis escalation language before any public content or workshop materials are used.

Choose the public positioning

Open Positioning & Brand Architecture. Select the primary title, short bio, audience, and niche language. Do not lead with hypnotherapy; lead with workplace wellbeing, mental health literacy, stress literacy, burnout prevention, and emotional organization.

Update LinkedIn manually

Use LinkedIn Authority Engine to update Dorina’s headline, about section, featured section, experience framing, and connection strategy. Nothing should be auto-posted without Dorina’s approval.

Approve the first 30 days of content

Use 12-Month LinkedIn Calendar. Start with Month 1 only. Dorina approves topics, tone, and sample posts before the graphics/design stage.

Set up the approval workflow

Open Approval & Automation Workflow. Use the workflow as the operating system: draft → review → Dorina approval → design → final approval → schedule/post.

Prepare the first workshop offer

Use Workshop System Blueprint and Workshop Exercises & Handouts. Customize the workshop for the audience before delivery.

Use the prompt library only after the strategy is approved

Open Premium Prompt Library. Use prompts to generate documents, posts, scripts, workshop assets, graphics briefs, and campaign material, but keep the approval gates active.

Upload to Claude Design for refinement

Open Claude Design Upload Prompt. Upload the website files and Markdown source folder listed there. Ask Claude Design to refine the visual system without changing the approved strategy or adding unsupported claims.
